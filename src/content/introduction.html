Ross on Wye Pub History Ale Trail

This Trail will guide you through the history of many of Ross's hostelries and taverns.

Ross on Wye, often referred to as a healthy town with superior air to the rest of the country it owns the label "The birth of Tourism"

You can just use this app to guide you round the pubs. Or you can register and all your photos,commentry route and timings will be there on the Pub History Ale Trail for you to see.
If there are a number of you then start a group trail so you can share photos and comments and view then all on the website along with where each of you were at any one time

To start the trail click on 'Start Trail' below, this will allow you to register and start a trail

Alternatively, just click on the tabs below to explore the app, you can use most of the functionality without registering if that's what you prefer
